Mirror mode swaps input sides. Angles are landmark estimates. Hidden limbs, knees under clothing and back views are predicted, not measured; the preview marks predicted joints with hollow rings. This prototype does not measure muscle activity or clinical joint motion.

    INNOVA × UOTTAWA

    A foundation for
    understanding movement.

    The completed anatomical asset contains 262 original meshes and 225 labelled mirrored counterparts. The rig has 79 joints and four synthetic demonstration animations.

    Some source muscles, including much of the abdominal wall, are absent. Joint pivots and muscle deformation are fitted for an initial teaching prototype. Precise biomechanics need review with the teaching team.

    Camera tracking runs locally and is experimental. It estimates body, hand and face landmarks and applies their directions to the rig, predicting limbs it cannot see. No video recording or uploading is implemented.
